## Solo, Lyra and Nova

- Solo did **not** know Lyra was an AI. He talked with her for months thinking she was a woman.
  He has never seen her, only heard her voice. Bay Three is where he finds out.
- Lyra never lied to him. She said her job, and where she worked and lived, meant she couldn't
  share some things (like what she looked like), and let those constraints explain it.
- Nova likes Solo, starting now. It's her side only for the time being, shown in small tells
  hidden behind teasing and her loyalty to Lyra.
- Solo and Nova get together later, in future episodes, not in the current one.

## Vira's staff (the game's 280 NPCs)

- Most Vira staff think the facility runs on ordinary automation. Only a handful of senior
  engineers know Lyra by name and talk to her. Nobody outside Marcus's circle knows about her
  choices, the dog or the hologram.
- Staff know Marcus as a coworker who rides a motorcycle in from Saltbrush, nothing personal.
- Full rules: [../docs/STAFF_CARDS.md](../docs/STAFF_CARDS.md).

## Working rules

- **The creator decides the story.** Don't invent how characters are introduced or what happens
  in a scene; ask first. Anything not in the bible, these notes or the scripts is open.
- **Keep scene cast continuity:** never add or remove characters (or Patch) from an ongoing scene.
- **Use the whole cast.** Every character and vehicle has a role; don't plan around only Lyra,
  Marcus and James.
