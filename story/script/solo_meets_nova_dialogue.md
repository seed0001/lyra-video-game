# Scene: Solo meets Nova (DRAFT)

Picks up after so7 (Solo walks into the lobby), so8 (Nova walks down the hallway from the glass server hall) and so9 (Solo's awed close-up).

## Geography

Lobby: glass entrance doors behind Solo, white cabinets with cyan light strips. Nova comes out of the concrete hallway that ends at the glass server-hall wall. They meet where the hallway opens into the lobby.

From there Nova leads him down a glass-walled corridor past the facility rooms. Juno is in a glass-walled workshop off the corridor, tinkering. The corridor ends at Bay Three, where Lyra's hologram stands beside James's rover with Marcus.
