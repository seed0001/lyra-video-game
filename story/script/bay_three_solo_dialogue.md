Picks up from so16 (Solo's POV from the doorway into Bay Three). so16 gets re-rolled so Marcus is bent over the rover's open front panel with Lyra watching him, which sets up "Marcus looks up from the vehicle."

## Geography (locked by so16)

The interior doorway is on the near side of the bay, facing the big open bay door and the desert mountains. The rover is parked center-left with its nose toward the doorway. Lyra's round platform is on the right, near the doorway side. Marcus starts at the rover. Nova and Solo walk in from the doorway and stop a few meters from the platform, with Solo nearest Lyra and Nova a step behind him.

The camera stays on the doorway side of the line between Solo and Lyra, so Solo always looks screen right toward Lyra and Lyra looks screen left toward Solo. Marcus is between them and the rover, so he looks screen left toward Solo and screen right toward Lyra.
