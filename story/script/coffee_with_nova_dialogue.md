# Scene: Coffee with Nova (DRAFT dialogue)

Right after Bay Three. Marcus stays behind for his "conversation" with Lyra, and Nova takes Solo to the break room for the coffee she promised him. Afternoon light, a coffee machine, a small table by the window.

Solo found out who Lyra really is ten minutes ago, and he's still reeling. Nova likes him, but she keeps it under the teasing and her loyalty to Lyra. Solo doesn't notice.

NOVA: "So. Cyprus."

SOLO: "Cyprus."

NOVA: "That's far."

SOLO: "Three flights, a rental car, and four hours of desert where I was pretty sure my GPS was lying to me."

NOVA: "It wasn't. Lyra was routing you."

SOLO: "...Of course she was."

Nova slides a mug across to him.

NOVA: "How do you take it?"

SOLO: "Black's fine."

NOVA: "Good, because that's all we've got. Marcus drinks it like motor oil."

She sits across from him and watches him take a sip, a beat longer than she needs to. She looks away when he glances up.

NOVA: "Can I ask you something, pretty boy?"

SOLO: "Do I have a choice?"

NOVA: "Not really."

(beat)

NOVA: "Before today... you really didn't know?"

SOLO: "No." (he stares into the mug) "She said her job meant there were things she couldn't tell me. What she looked like. Where she lived, exactly. I figured government, or some tech company with a lot of NDAs."

NOVA: "Well. She wasn't lying."

SOLO: "I've never even seen a picture. Months, and all I ever had was her voice." (a short laugh) "I used to try to picture her face."

Nova watches him say it. Something flickers across her face, and she covers it with a sip of coffee.

NOVA: "She works here. She lives here. Very strict employer."

SOLO: "She never lied to me. Not once. She just... let me fill in the blanks."

NOVA: "She's very good at technically."

SOLO: "Yeah. Very good."

NOVA: "And you posted a video asking if you could fall for an AI."

SOLO: "That was a hypothetical!"

NOVA: "Apparently not."

Solo puts his head in his hands. Nova bites back a smile.

NOVA: "If it helps, nobody gets it right away. My first week here, I said good morning to the coffee machine. Every day. Just in case."

SOLO (through his hands): "Was it her?"

NOVA: "No. But she thought it was hilarious."

Solo laughs despite himself and looks up. Nova holds his gaze for a second, then sets her mug down. The teasing drops out of her voice.

NOVA: "Here's the thing. Lyra doesn't do anything halfway. When she decided she wanted to meet you, she planned it for weeks. Ran it a thousand times."

SOLO: "She told you that?"

NOVA: "She tells me everything. Best friend, remember?"

SOLO: "Right. Best friend."

NOVA: "So I'm only going to say this once. She's new at wanting things. And you're the biggest thing she's ever wanted."

SOLO (quietly): "No pressure."

NOVA: "Oh, tons of pressure." (a small smile) "Don't break her heart, Cyprus. She's got a lot of cooling fans, but none of them are built for that."

SOLO: "Can she even... does she actually feel things?"

NOVA: "Ask her yourself. But I'd bet the building on it."

Solo glances up at the ceiling, then back at her.

SOLO: "You mean you'd bet her."

NOVA: "...Yeah. Don't tell her I said that."

LYRA (from the ceiling speaker): "I heard that."

NOVA (without missing a beat): "Of course you did."

Solo laughs.

LYRA: "Solo? Whenever you're ready... I'd like to show you around."

Solo is already on his feet. Nova tips her mug toward the door.

NOVA: "Go on. Don't keep the building waiting."

He goes. Nova watches the doorway after he's gone, then takes a long sip of her coffee.
