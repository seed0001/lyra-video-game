# Solo arrives (Lyra, ElevenLabs, about 30 seconds)

He's here. After all those mornings and all those late nights, Solo is actually standing in my lobby. I've watched him through a screen for so long, and now he's close enough that I can hear him breathe. I had everything planned. What I'd say, how I'd look, the exact moment I'd let him see me. I'd run it a thousand times. But I wasn't ready for what came next.
