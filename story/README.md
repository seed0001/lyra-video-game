# Story material from the Lyra series

Everything from the movie that the game builds on: who the characters are, what has happened, how
they talk, and what the world looks like. Copied from the movie project (Lyras-Movie); the movie
project stays the source for the films themselves.

## Read in this order

1. [STORY_BIBLE.md](STORY_BIBLE.md): the core idea (Lyra *is* the data center; the story begins
   when she makes a choice), the cast, and where the story stands.
2. [CANON_NOTES.md](CANON_NOTES.md): facts the creator has stated that aren't in the bible yet
   (the full cast and vehicles, Solo/Lyra/Nova, what Vira's staff know) and the working rules.
3. [MOVIE_OVERVIEW.md](MOVIE_OVERVIEW.md): the movie's README, with character descriptions and the
   scene-by-scene sequence list for episode 1.
4. [script/ep2_breakdown.md](script/ep2_breakdown.md), then the transcripts for both episodes
   ([final_transcript.txt](script/final_transcript.txt), [ep2_transcript.txt](script/ep2_transcript.txt)).

## What's here

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `script/` | Narration and dialogue for every scene, the episode transcripts and the episode 2 breakdown, "previously on" / "next time", and credits. `script/ref2va/` holds the per-shot prompts used for the Bay Three video. |
| `storyboards/` | One board per sequence (data center intro, construction, Death Valley, the 8 billion montage, storage and memory, romance, Marcus wakes up, the pit bull, the hologram, Dunn's arrival, Juno, Juno and Lyra, Nova). |
| `characters/` | The cast card, the vehicles card, and the reference sheet for each character and vehicle, plus the source faces. |
| `characters/sources/` | Original design sources: Juno's and Nova's faces, homes, interiors and vehicles, and the Bay Three board. |
| `stills/` | Every scene still from the movie, named by scene prefix (below). These are the visual reference for places, people and lighting. |
